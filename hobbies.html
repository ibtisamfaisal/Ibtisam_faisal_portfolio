My Favourite Online Game

Valorant

Valorant is an online multiplayer tactical shooting game developed by Riot Games. It is a team-based game where players use different characters and their special abilities to defeat the opposing team. I enjoy playing Valorant because it improves teamwork, communication, strategy and quick decision-making.

My Favourite Physical Game

Football

Football is my favourite physical sport. It is played between two teams, with each team trying to score goals by putting the ball into the opponent's net. I enjoy playing football because it improves physical fitness, teamwork, coordination and discipline. It also helps me stay active and healthy.
